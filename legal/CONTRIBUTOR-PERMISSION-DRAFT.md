# Contributor permission — DRAFT, opt-in only

This is a proposed agreement, **NOT IN FORCE** and not accepted by submitting
an issue or pull request. Existing contributors retain their copyright and
existing independently granted rights. No copyright assignment is presumed.

For adoption, record contributor identity, contact, employer authority if
needed, exact commit/file scope, agreement version, date and explicit written
acceptance. Keep personal acceptance records privately; publish only agreed
attribution and a nonsecret permission reference in the rights inventory.

Proposed grant: the contributor retains ownership and grants Mach Hong Hai a
nonexclusive, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free permission to reproduce,
modify, build, use, distribute and sublicense the specifically identified
contribution, including within official HaizFlow releases under the approved
source-available and application terms or other terms. The grant is irrevocable
to the extent permitted by law, except for breach of this agreement. It does
not grant rights in unlisted third-party material or require moral-rights
waivers. Existing licenses and mandatory rights remain effective.

Any patent permission must be limited to claims the contributor controls that
are necessarily infringed by the identified contribution; its final language
and termination conditions require separate owner/legal approval. No broad
patent indemnity is assumed by this draft.

The contributor must identify third-party material, license restrictions and
required notices, and confirm authority to grant the identified rights. The
maintainer must preserve attribution and review compatibility before merging
into a restricted release. This draft is not evidence that earlier contributors
have agreed to relicensing.
