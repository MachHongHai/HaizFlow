Before installing HaizFlow

This installer contains the application. After launch, open Settings → Resource packs to install the models you need. Recognition, local translation and video processing run on your computer. Online services require Internet access and may require their own API keys.

Data, settings and the project index are stored in the runtime folder under the installation location. Resource storage can be moved in Settings. Installing over an existing copy preserves its data; uninstalling offers a choice to retain data for a later reinstall.

The next page displays the HaizFlow license. Libraries, codecs and models have separate licenses supplied with the application. The OmniVoice checkpoint has a non-commercial restriction. You need the appropriate rights to your videos, music and voice samples.

This page explains installation and use; it does not replace the full licenses.
